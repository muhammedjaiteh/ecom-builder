Sanndikaa Design Principles
Principle 1
Merchant trust is more important than novelty.

Never sacrifice credibility for creativity.

Principle 2
Niche authenticity beats visual experimentation.

A bakery should feel like a bakery.

An abaya boutique should feel like an abaya boutique.

An electronics store should feel like an electronics store.

Principle 3
Never invent merchant facts.

Use only real merchant data.

When facts are absent, be honest.

Principle 4
Mobile-first always.

Most customers arrive through:

WhatsApp
Facebook
Instagram
Direct product links
Every decision must work on mobile first.

Principle 5
Conversion beats decoration.

Prioritize:

Product discovery
Trust
WhatsApp conversion
Merchant clarity
before visual effects.

Principle 6
Regeneration must feel meaningful.

Changing:

colors
labels
headlines
is not enough.

Merchants must perceive generated storefronts as genuinely different.

Principle 7
Layout variation matters more than copy variation.

Merchants judge:

hierarchy
composition
product presentation
before they judge copy.

Principle 8
Protect successful fixes.

Never reintroduce:

category leakage
archetype leakage
inappropriate concepts
mobile overflow bugs
Principle 9
Use the smallest effective change.

Investigate first.

Fix second.

Redesign last.

Principle 10
Architecture should create freedom.

Prefer:

layout strategies
composable variants
reusable systems
over template explosions.
